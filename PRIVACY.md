# Kithova — Privacy Policy

_Effective date: [ADD DATE BEFORE PUBLISHING]_

Kithova ("we," "us," or "the app") is a trip-planning app for families and groups
traveling together. We built it so your data stays yours. This policy explains what
the app does and does not do with your information.

> **Draft notice:** this is a starting draft to review and adapt — **not legal advice.**
> Confirm it matches your final data practices and have it reviewed before you publish it.

## The short version

- Your trips, families, expenses, and plans are stored **locally on your device**. We
  do not run a server, we have no accounts, and we keep no copy of your data.
- We **do not sell, rent, or share** your personal data.
- We use **no advertising networks** and **no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs**.

## What we store, and where

Everything you create — trips, traveler and family profiles, activities, expenses, and
settlement calculations — is saved **on your device** using the device's local storage.
It is not transmitted to us or to any Kithova server (there isn't one). Removing the app
deletes this data.

## Third-party services the app calls

To provide certain features, the app sends limited, specific requests to outside services.
We send only what a feature needs, and — because the app has no accounts — these requests
are not tied to your identity:

- **Google Places API** — when you search for or add a place, your search text and an
  approximate location/coordinates are sent to Google to return matching places, photos,
  and details. Governed by Google's Privacy Policy.
- **Wikipedia / Wikimedia** — to show a destination photo, the app requests public article
  data for the destination name. Governed by the Wikimedia Foundation Privacy Policy.
- **Booking and similar links** — some "Book" actions open external websites in your
  browser; those sites have their own privacy policies (see "Affiliate links" below).

## What we do NOT do

- No user accounts or login (in this version).
- No advertising networks.
- No third-party analytics or behavior-tracking SDKs.
- No sale or sharing of your personal information.

## Affiliate links

Some outbound booking links may be affiliate links, which means we may earn a commission
if you book through them. This does not change the price you pay, and it does not send your
trip data to us.

## Children's privacy

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal
information from them. Any family-member names or ages you choose to enter remain on your
device.

## Your choices

Because your data lives on your device, you control it: edit or delete trips in the app, or
delete the app to remove everything.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time; the effective date above will change to reflect
