---

**Kithova is the only app built for multi-family trips: plan it together, split it fairly, and keep
it forever.** We solve the one thing no one else does — splitting group travel costs *per family* —
then own the whole trip lifecycle: **plan → split → remember.**

### The problem
A multi-family trip runs across **four apps that don't talk**: WhatsApp (chat), Wanderlog (plan),
Splitwise (split), Photos (remember).
- **Money is unfair:** every splitter divides *per person* — but lodging splits by *rooms* and
  transit by *family size*. No tool models the family. Someone eats the math or the cost.
- **The trip evaporates:** months later it lives in a dead group chat and in date/face-based phone
  "memories" that can't answer *"the Smoky Mountains trip with the Garcias — and who owed what."*

### The solution & moat
One data spine — **families × days × places × money × photos** — that no one else has:
- **Splitwise** = money, no context. **Wanderlog** = plan, then silence. **Photos** = pics, no trip,
  no money. **Kithova** = all three on one model.
- **Lock-in:** once a family's trips (plan + settlement + photos) live here, leaving loses the
  archive. Competitors hold one slice; we hold the whole trip.
- **Trust:** the split engine is **deterministic — AI never touches the money** (test-locked).

### Market (bottoms-up, US group/multi-family trips/yr)
**TAM ~20M trips · SAM ~8M (shared-lodging, organizer-led) · SOM yr-3 ~0.3–0.5M.**
ICP = the **trip captain** (reunions · multi-family Disney/cabin/beach · destination weddings ·
sports & church groups). Each trip ≈ 3 families / ~10–12 people.

### Business model — free core, money downstream
- **Free forever:** plan + profiles + **the split & settlement** + Trip Check. *This is the viral loop.*
- **Revenue (in sequence):** ① **affiliate lodging** — the big lever; multi-room basket ~3× a solo
  booking; APIs pay *us* (~4–6%), free to the user, approval-gated not money-gated. ② per-trip Pro/AI
  (~$6.99). ③ premium memories tier (later).

### Unit economics (per *completed* trip = ≥2 families that settled)
- **Blended mature ARPU ~$30–40** (≈$2,500 lodging × 5% × 25% book-through + Pro attach).
- **CAC divisor:** the settlement recap is shared to the *other ~3 families* → every trip markets us
  to several households at once. Near-zero-CAC channels: organizer communities + "how to split group
  travel costs" SEO. **Target LTV:CAC > 3:1** (LTV ~$100+/captain), compounding with the share loop.

### Traction we're proving → Series A
**North-star: completed multi-family trips reaching settlement.** Watch: settlement rate → virality
(K: share-outs → new captains) → repeat captains & W4 retention → (Phase 3) Book-tap rate.

### The ask
**Seed ~$1.0–1.5M, 18–24 mo runway** → Phase-2 backend (auth, **keys server-side**, catalog DB,
**every member contributes**), reference-based memories (host only the shared recap), and affiliate
integration to switch on revenue.

> *Kithova already built the hard, trustworthy moat nobody else builds — fair multi-family money.
> This round turns that wedge into the place every group keeps its whole trip — plan, money, and
